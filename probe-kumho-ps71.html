Four seasons. More miles.

The all-new Road Venture HT delivers the best of all worlds – extended tread life, exceptional wet performance, comfort and quiet on-road and EV compatibility. A one-of-a-kind in its’ category with exceptional snow performance and a three-peak mountain certification, it’s designed to perform at its peak wherever the road takes you, in any condition.

Driving the future of EV tires

Enjoy a quieter, smoother ride with tires built specifically for electric CUVs and SUVs. Designed for comfort, durability and efficiency, they feature advanced K-Silent noise-reducing technology giving you premium performance without the premium price.

The power to rough it

These tires feature an aggressive tread pattern that delivers unstoppable off-road traction. Stone ejector bars protect against damage and help keep tires debris-free, while the durable design ensures a smooth, stable ride and confident handling on every type of road.
